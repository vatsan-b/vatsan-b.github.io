I am Srivatsan, and this is my personal website.

I build adaptive hardware that helps robots work reliably in the messy real world, and allow them do more than their current physical limits allow. My work includes improving soft pneumatic network actuators, designing and characterizing metamaterial-based soft grippers, and integrating flexible electronics and developing platforms for underwater grasping and robotic harvesting. I’m interested in hardware that can adapt, survive real-world chaos, and give the software and controls more room to work.
