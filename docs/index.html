Designing adaptive robotic hardware that help robots work reliably in the messy real world.

My goal is to enhance the capabilities of robots by building hardware that overcomes existing physical limitations. By integrating compliant mechanisms, designing torsion-resistant metamaterials, and embedding flexible tactile sensors. When the hardware is smart, adaptive, and structurally resilient, the software boundary expands.
